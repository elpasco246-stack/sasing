Welcome to Healthy Study Habits

Introduction

This is a short sample course built in the style of an eXeLearning HTML5 export. It shows the most common content blocks (called iDevices in eXe): objectives, free text, a multiple-choice quiz, true/false questions, collapsible content, and a reflection activity.

Estimated time: 10 minutes.

How to use this course

  1. Use the menu on the left (or the Next button) to move between pages.
  2. Pages you have visited are marked with a tick and the progress bar fills up.
  3. Quiz answers are checked instantly. You can retry as often as you like.
