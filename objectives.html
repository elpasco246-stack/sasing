Objectives

Learning objectives

By the end of this course you will be able to:

  • Explain why short, spaced study sessions beat long cramming sessions.
  • Plan a weekly study schedule with realistic goals.
  • Use at least two active-recall techniques.
  • Recognise the role of sleep and breaks in memory.

Prior knowledge

No prior knowledge is needed. This course suits students of any age or level.
